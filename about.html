About Precision Engineers

Engineering confidence from drawing to site.

Precision Engineers is a Guwahati-based structural engineering consultancy focused on practical design, dependable supervision, clear estimates, and approval-ready documentation.

Founder Hiran Mayee Kalita

Committed to safe structures, clear communication, and project decisions that work on site.

Founder Led

Technical work with a personal sense of responsibility.

Precision Engineers works closely with clients, builders, and project teams to make structural decisions easier to understand and easier to execute.

The consultancy supports projects through structural design and drawing, site supervision, preparation of estimates, and building permission requirements. The work is guided by a simple belief: a good structure should be safe, practical, and coordinated before it reaches the site.
