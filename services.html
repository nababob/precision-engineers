Services

Structural clarity for planning, approval, and construction.

Precision Engineers supports projects through the technical work that keeps construction organized: design drawings, supervision, estimates, and building permission documentation.

01

Structural Design & Drawing

Design and drawing support for structures that need strength, stability, coordination, and practical construction logic.

  • RCC structural planning and design support
  • Column, beam, slab, footing, and stair detailing
  • Construction-ready structural drawings
  • Coordination with architectural requirements
02

Site Supervision

Site review and supervision support to help construction work follow design intent and quality expectations.

  • Review of reinforcement placement and structural details
  • Guidance during critical construction stages
  • Coordination with contractors and site teams
  • Practical checks before concrete work
03

Preparation of Estimate

Estimate preparation to support budget planning, material clarity, and smarter decisions before construction begins.

  • Quantity calculation support
  • Material and work item breakdowns
  • Cost planning for construction decisions
  • Clear documentation for project discussion
04

Building Permission

Drawing and documentation support for clients preparing building permission submissions and related requirements.

  • Permission drawing support
  • Document preparation assistance
  • Guidance on required project information
  • Coordination-ready technical output
How Work Begins

A simple intake keeps the project moving.

Share the site location, available drawings, project size, service requirement, and timeline. Precision Engineers will guide the next step based on the project stage.

Call

Discuss the project requirement and expected scope.

Share Details

Provide site, drawing, dimension, and approval information.

Review

Clarify feasibility, deliverables, and technical needs.

Prepare

Create drawings, estimates, or documents as required.

Support

Continue with site or approval assistance when needed.
